FAQ
Does Chargie show my real battery?

Yes. It reads your device's real system battery level and never fabricates a number. A not-charging preview is always clearly labelled.

Does it auto-play on my Lock Screen?

No. iOS doesn't allow that. Chargie's Live Activity is one you start, and it guides you to set up an optional Shortcuts automation for charge scenes.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No. This version is completely free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Does it collect my data?

No. No accounts, no analytics, no trackers, no third-party SDKs. Your preferences stay on your device.

Can Chargie set my wallpaper for me?

No — and no app can. iOS doesn't allow it. Chargie saves the image you made to your photo library (add-only access: it can never read your existing photos) and shows you the exact steps in Settings ▸ Wallpaper to apply it.
